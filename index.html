ICT — INNER CIRCLE TRADER

Liquidity & smart-money timing

Order blocks, fair value gaps, liquidity sweeps, and killzone timing — we can automate ICT-style concepts so the bot waits for the same setups you'd wait for by hand.

SMC — SMART MONEY CONCEPTS

From a conversation to a working, automated bot.

    Consultation
