What we can automate

Strategy frameworks we build around.

These are some of the concepts we most often translate into code. If you trade something else entirely, that's fine too — the framework below is a starting point, not a limit.

ICT — INNER CIRCLE TRADER

Liquidity & smart-money timing

Order blocks, fair value gaps, liquidity sweeps, and killzone timing — we can encode ICT-style concepts so the bot waits for the same setups you'd wait for by hand.

SMC — SMART MONEY CONCEPTS

Structure-led entries

Break of structure, change of character, supply and demand zones — SMC-based bots read market structure shifts before committing to a direction.

MMC — MARKET MAKER CONCEPTS

Reading engineered moves

Modeling how liquidity gets engineered and swept before the "real" move — useful for traders whose edge comes from anticipating stop-hunts and reversals.

How it works

From a conversation to a working bot.

  1. Consultation

    You tell us how you trade — strategy, instruments, risk tolerance, and what "automated" should actually mean for you.

  2. Strategy mapping

    We translate your rules — ICT, SMC, MMC, or your own system — into precise, testable logic before a single line of production code is written.

  3. Build & backtest

    The bot is built, then stress-tested against historical data so you can see how it would have behaved before it trades a single dollar live.

  4. Delivery & support

    You get the finished bot plus direct support from the founder while you get it running on your own account.
